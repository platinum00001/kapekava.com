About Cape Kava

1

What is kava?

Kava is a drink made from the root of the kava plant, shared for centuries in Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga, Samoa and Hawaii. It's traditionally served from a wide wooden bowl and passed around in coconut-shell cups as a way to welcome guests and bring people together.

2

Our idea

A calm, alcohol-free place to hang out on the Cape: lounge nights, chef pairings, golf meetups, travel stories and community fundraisers, all built around the tradition of sharing a bowl.
